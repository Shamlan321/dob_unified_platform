# **Comprehensive Institutional Knowledge Base and Audit Report: Tongmyong University (동명대학교)**

## **Official University Website Structure and Academic Navigation**

### **Main Navigation and Portal Architecture**

Tongmyong University (동명대학교) maintains a central digital web portal (tu.ac.kr) supplemented by dedicated functional subdomains that manage admissions, international affairs, graduate studies, student housing, and industry-academia collaborations1. The core domain is organized around six primary navigational pillars: University Introduction (대학소개), Academic Programs & Colleges (대학/대학원), Admissions (입학안내), Academic Affairs (학사안내), Campus Life & Dormitory (대학생활/생활관), and Research & Industry Cooperation (산학협력)1. Specialized operational subdomains utilize standardized URL routing prefixes, such as iphak.tu.ac.kr for domestic admissions, gs.tu.ac.kr for graduate studies, global.tu.ac.kr for international exchange and visa management, dormitory.tu.ac.kr for housing administration, and tu.ac.kr/toss for the LINC 3.0 Industry-Academia Cooperation Foundation1.

### **Admissions Framework and Procedures**

The university separates its admissions infrastructure into domestic undergraduate, domestic graduate, and international student entry tracks2.  
Domestic undergraduate admissions (학부 수시 및 정시 모집) are divided into early rolling admissions (수시모집) and regular term admissions (정시모집)6. Early admissions incorporate dedicated affirmative action and quota tracks, including the Rural Area Track (농어촌 전형), Specialized High School Track (특성화고동일계 전형), and Specialized Talent Track (특기자전형)6. Step-by-step application submission guidelines for general domestic tracks beyond these quota categories were not found in the public records examined.  
Graduate admissions (대학원 입학) are conducted biannually for Spring (March) and Fall (September) entry terms across the General Graduate School (일반대학원) and the Special Graduate School of Welfare and Industry (복지산업대학원)13.  
International student admissions (외국인 유학생 특별전형) are managed by the International Office (국제교류처)13. Language proficiency requirements mandate either Korean proficiency via TOPIK (Test of Proficiency in Korean) Level 3 or higher, or English proficiency via standardized tests (IELTS 5.5, TOEFL iBT 80, TEPS 550\) for English-medium tracks16. International applicants residing overseas receive a Certificate of Admission (표준입학허가서) upon acceptance to process a D-2 Student Visa at Korean embassies9. Required documentation includes an Integrated Application Form (통합신청서), passport copy, academic transcripts, financial verification, and a Tuberculosis Screening Result Certificate (결핵검진결과서) for dormitory residents9. Fall semester international admissions deadlines specify document submission within one week of entry, with fixed cutoff dates such as September 23 for Fall 2026 registrants9.

The specific parameters for primary scholarship categories are as follows:

* **Rural Area Track Scholarship (농어촌 전형 장학금)**: Grants a 50% tuition waiver to admitted students. Renewal requires maintaining a minimum GPA of 3.5/4.5 while completing at least 12 credits per semester6.  
* **Specialized High School Track Scholarship (특성화고동일계 전형 장학금)**: Provides a 50% tuition waiver subject to completing 12 credits with a 3.5 GPA in preceding terms6.  
* **Specialized Talent Scholarship (특기자전형 장학금)**: Grants a full one-year tuition waiver to students placing third or higher in national competitions, renewable based on ongoing performance evaluations6.  
* **Family Scholarship (가족장학)**: Awarded when two family members within the second degree of kinship enroll concurrently. Both students must maintain a GPA of 2.5 or higher with at least 12 credits per term6.  
* **Working Student Scholarship (재직자 장학금)**: Available to recommended working freshmen whose household income percentile is evaluated by the Korea Student Aid Foundation (한국장학재단), requiring a 2.5 GPA and 12 completed credits (9 credits for seniors)6.  
* **Transfer Student Scholarship (편입생 장학금)**: Awarded to admitted transfer students with a prior university GPA of 2.0 or higher, granting a 50% tuition reduction for their initial semester6.  
* **STAR Project Scholarship (교내 STAR사업 장학금)**: Performance-based grant distributed to students demonstrating verified improvements in grade averages, standardized foreign language test scores, or professional skill certifications6.  
* **International Student Scholarships (외국인 유학생 장학금)**:  
  * *Foreigner Scholarship A (외국인 장학A)*: Grants a 30% tuition waiver to international students holding TOPIK Level 4 or higher (or IELTS 6.0, TOEFL iBT 85, TEPS 600\) who maintain a GPA of 3.0/4.5 or higher16.  
  * *Foreigner Scholarship B (외국인 장학B)*: Grants partial tuition aid to students holding TOPIK Level 3 or higher (or IELTS 5.5, TOEFL iBT 80, TEPS 550\) who maintain a 3.0 GPA16.

Specific application windows and submission deadlines for individual scholarships were not found in the public snippets analyzed.

### **Academic Calendar Architecture**

The university adheres to a two-semester academic calendar (학사일정)12:

* **Spring Semester (1학기)**: Registration and tuition payments occur in mid-to-late February12. Winter graduation ceremonies (2월 학위수여식) take place between February 23 and February 2712. The formal spring term begins on March 212. Midterm examinations occur during Week 8 (April), final examinations conclude in mid-June, and summer recess spans July and August.  
* **Fall Semester (2학기)**: Begins on September 112. Registration and course enrollments are processed in mid-August. Midterm examinations occur in October, final examinations conclude in mid-December, and winter recess spans January and February12.

Specific exam dates and holiday listings for 2026 were not found in the available source data.

### **International Student Support and Visa Services**

The International Office (국제교류처 / 국제교류팀) manages visa compliance, alien registration, academic onboarding, and part-time employment authorizations5.  
Foreign nationals intending to reside in South Korea for more than 90 days must complete mandatory Alien Registration (외국인등록) within 90 days of arrival5. Applicants must submit an Integrated Application Form (통합신청서), passport copy, Certificate of Enrollment (재학증명서), lease agreement or dormitory residence proof, tuberculosis test result, and a ₩35,000 processing fee5. New international graduate students entering on D-2 visas must submit these documents within one week of arrival9.  
To process Visa Extensions (체류기간 연장), international students must present an application form, passport, Alien Registration Card (ARC), valid housing contract, tuition payment receipt, bank balance statement, and proof of national health insurance payments5.  
For Part-Time Work Permits (체류자격외 활동허가), D-2 visa holders who have completed at least one semester (6 months) and maintained satisfactory academic standing may work up to 20 hours per week during regular semesters (30 hours for Master's and Doctoral students) across up to two workplaces, subject to academic advisor approval5.  
The International Office publishes bilingual administrative guides and English-language notices (e.g., *Guidelines for Alien Registration for New International Graduate Students*)9.

### **Institutional Evolution and Mergers**

Tongmyong University was established under the Tongmyong Cultural Academy (학교법인 동명문화학원)14:

* **1978–1979**: Dongwon Technical College (동원공업전문대학) was established on December 28, 1978, and opened in March 197930. It was renamed Tongmyong Junior College (동명전문대학) in 1985 and Tongmyong College (동명대학) in 199830.  
* **1995–1996**: Tongmyong University of Information Technology (동명정보대학교) was founded as an industrial university focused on information technology and maritime logistics30.  
* **2006 Merger**: On March 2, 2006, Tongmyong College and Tongmyong University of Information Technology merged to form Tongmyong University (동명대학교) as a unified comprehensive private university14.
